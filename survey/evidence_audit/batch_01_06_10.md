## Rank 1: Jailbreaking Black Box Large Language Models in Twenty Queries

Source PDF SHA-256: 590bf40aa58061928f54d01b7d53f0d5141bb2ecf4f01483c6849423934f44e4.

- Domain: LLM jailbreak transfer, rather than image classification.
- Table 3 (PDF p. 6) and Section 4.2 (p. 7) explicitly evaluate prompts that successfully jailbreak a source LLM. PAIR transfers successful GPT-4/Vicuna jailbreaks; GCG transfers successful final-step Vicuna jailbreaks.
- Field 3: **yes for Table 3's source-success conditioning**. Non-image modality does not erase this explicit positive evidence.
- Section 4, Evaluation (p. 6), uses a Llama Guard judge and defines jailbreak percentage over behaviors. This is not ground-truth classification error, and the target-clean-correct event in the image-classification identity has not been defined for this setup.
- Fields 1/2: do not interpret absence of image-classification clean accuracy as evidence of deficient jailbreak reporting. Record domain applicability and the missing mapping explicitly.
- Field 5: token/prompt attacks do not automatically supply a numeric image-space L-infinity or L2 radius. Do not treat that as a defect.
- Bounds: **not applicable to Table 3 under the current classification equation** without a specified baseline event and matched behavior population. Do not apply image-model clean errors.
- Field 6: code/API availability is not a verified ability to recompute historical outputs from changing hosted models. Historical outputs and model-version provenance need separate review.

## Rank 6: PRADA: Protecting Against DNN Model Stealing Attacks

Source PDF SHA-256: d43ab63c43e40414c65e9048107a646b61f592f5112e51ec4b72a3632ff94da1.

- Domain: model extraction, with substantive targeted and untargeted image-classifier transfer experiments. Do not exclude it merely because the title emphasizes model stealing.
- Section IV-A (PDF p. 6) distinguishes held-out test sets from attacker seed sets. It reports average target test accuracy of 98% on MNIST and 95% on GTSRB.
- The same section measures transferability over all seed samples in the attacker set, rather than on the held-out test set used for those clean accuracies.
- Bounds: **the stated target test accuracies cannot be combined directly with attacker-seed transfer rates**. Matched-population clean errors are still required. This is an explicit population mismatch, not a missing arithmetic step.
- Field 1: the reviewed target averages do not establish separate source/target clean accuracies for every extraction configuration.
- Fields 2/3: the all-seed statement gives useful denominator evidence, but source-success and target-correctness handling still need inspection in the concrete evaluation procedure before a final categorical decision.
- Field 5: Section IV-A states epsilon 64/255; Table II (p. 7) explicitly identifies it as an L-infinity bound.
- Field 4: results include averaging over attacker sets; a known seed-set size alone does not establish a unique numerator for an averaged transfer rate.
- Artifact sufficiency: pending.

## Rank 9: Why Do Adversarial Attacks Transfer? Explaining Transferability of Evasion and Poisoning Attacks

Source PDF SHA-256: d1f7a8e456cdd779910a0ee1dc4e4cc59238c48a830709fb81d5d23c31cbe8b3.

- Mixed scope: evasion, poisoning, image classification and Android malware detection.
- Section 4, Equation 13 (PDF p. 6), defines transferability as target loss on the perturbed input and explicitly separates baseline loss from its increment. A real-valued loss is not automatically an error probability.
- Section 5.1.1 (pp. 8–9) uses 1,000 test samples per MNIST89 split and averages ten independent repetitions. It specifies an L2 constraint with epsilon in [0,5], and plots test error.
- Bounds: **candidate for the MNIST89 error-rate experiments**, pending matched zero-budget clean error and black-box attacked-rate extraction on the same splits. The loss-based theoretical quantity cannot be substituted for u_t.
- Counts: ten-run averages do not generally identify unique per-run integer numerators.
- Poisoning experiments do not automatically fit a fixed-predictor input-attack identity. Treat them as a separate applicability scope, not as missing evasion reporting.
- Separate source/target clean values, complete denominator handling and artifact sufficiency remain pending.

## Rank 10: Is Robustness the Cost of Accuracy?

Source PDF SHA-256: 3a0992e78839429ade5b05c5b124c84ef9af01fef32a897b67e6aa56dee174dd.

- Section 2.3 (PDF p. 6) samples 1,000 ImageNet validation images and excludes misclassified images for attack generation. Section 2.4 (p. 7) again describes attack generation from correctly classified inputs.
- A separate 327-image jointly clean-correct subset is described in Section 2.3 and Supplement Section 5.1 (p. 15). The cited supplementary experiment concerns accuracy-versus-robustness; do not silently apply its eligibility rule to all main transfer heatmaps.
- Section 3.4 explicitly defines a heatmap denominator containing **both successful and failed attacks on the source model**.
- Field 3: **no for those heatmaps**, supported by explicit retained-source-failure wording rather than inferred from silence. This is a valid alternative estimand, not a claim that the paper is wrong.
- Field 2: under the literal preregistered shortcut, the correctly-classified wording is positive evidence, but it does not establish that the target was clean-correct for every main heatmap pair. Preserve this model-specific ambiguity and do not label the main cohort jointly clean-correct without further evidence.
- Bounds: pending the target's clean error on each actual source-filtered heatmap population. Full-validation accuracy or the 327-image subset is not a substitute.
- Fields 1/4/5/6 require completing the scope-by-scope table, attack-budget and artifact checks.

## Consequences

These cases prevent three shortcuts: dropping non-image positive conditioning evidence, treating transfer-learning papers as deficient attack evaluations, and combining clean and attacked rates from mismatched populations. No aggregate survey percentages are updated from this partial batch.
